SnapGrab

Save any public Instagram post to your device

Paste a link to a post, reel, story or profile, then pick the photo or video quality you want.

    How it works

    1. Open Instagram and copy the link of a public post, reel or story.
    2. Paste it above and select Get download links.
    3. Choose a quality and select Download. If the file opens in a new tab, long-press or right-click and save it.
    Only download content you own or have permission to use. This site is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
